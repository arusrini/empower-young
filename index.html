Talent is Universal.

Opportunity Should Be, Too.

We are a global initiative dedicated to unlocking the potential of bright, underserved youth by providing pathways to higher education and meaningful careers.

A world where every young person has the chance to achieve their full potential, regardless of their background. We believe that talent is everywhere, and we are committed to creating pathways to success for aspiring youth worldwide.

To ignite the spark of potential in underserved youth by providing them with the tools, resources, and support they need to pursue higher education and build successful careers. We are dedicated to creating a global community of empowered young leaders who will drive positive change in their communities and beyond.

Empower Young Foundation Inc. is a global organization built upon the proven and successful model of the Swadha Foundation in India. In addition to scaling this impactful model for worldwide initiatives, we are proud to partner with and support Swadha's incredible work on the ground. As a key partner, we act as a vital channel for resources and funding to expand their reach and deepen their impact, ensuring that more young people in India have the chance to build a brighter future.

About Us

Who We Are: A Global Movement for Youth Empowerment

Our Story

Empower Young Foundation Inc. was born from a simple, powerful idea: that a single opportunity can change a life, a family, and a community for generations. We saw the incredible success of the Swadha Foundation in India, which has been transforming the lives of rural youth since its inception. Inspired by their impactful model, we decided to take this proven approach to the global stage.

Our Philosophy

We believe in the power of education to break the cycle of poverty. We focus on identifying high-potential students from disadvantaged backgrounds who have the drive and determination to succeed but lack the resources. By providing them with comprehensive support, from financial aid to mentorship, we empower them to not only complete their education but also to become leaders and changemakers.

Our Team

We are a diverse team of passionate individuals from around the world, united by a common belief in the power of youth. Our team includes experienced educators, business leaders, and social entrepreneurs, all dedicated to creating a more equitable world.

Our Model

Our Three-Pillar Approach to Empowerment Our holistic approach, modeled after the successful work of the Swadha Foundation, is designed to nurture talent and unlock opportunities for the next generation of leaders. It is built on three key pillars:

ASCEND: From Potential to Pursuit

We identify promising young individuals from marginalized communities through a rigorous and unbiased selection process. We then provide them with the financial and academic support necessary to access higher education, removing the barriers that stand in their way and helping them ascend to new heights.

ACCELERATE: From Education to Expertise

We go beyond traditional education by offering personalized mentorship from industry professionals, skill-building workshops, and leadership development programs. This helps our scholars accelerate their personal and professional growth, preparing them for the demands of the global workforce.

ACHIEVE: From Graduation to Gainful Employment

We connect our graduates with meaningful employment opportunities, empowering them to achieve financial independence and become catalysts for change within their own families and communities. We measure our success by the success of those we serve.

Our Impact

Creating a Legacy of Change, One Life at a Time​

The Ripple Effect of Empowerment

Our impact extends far beyond the individual. When one young person is empowered through education, the positive effects ripple outwards, transforming families, communities, and future generations. The graduates of our programs often become the highest earners in their families, inspiring others to follow in their footsteps and creating a virtuous cycle of empowerment.
